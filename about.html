Meet Glen

Hi, I'm Glen Patterson.

I'm the founder of Trifecta Advisor, the co-founder of WorkSchool, and a business and professional coach based in New York City. I studied at Columbia University's School of International and Public Affairs.

I work with two kinds of people: professionals who want to build a side business without quitting their 9-5, and small business owners who are working hard but not seeing consistent profit.

My approach is simple. Get your first customers before you build something big. Know your numbers. Build systems instead of chaos. And keep improving, because small changes compound.
